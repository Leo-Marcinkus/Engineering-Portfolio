# Electromagnetics, Waves, and Fields Projects

This directory contains technical projects focused on applied
electromagnetic theory, field modeling, and wave propagation analysis.

The work emphasizes analytical modeling, numerical simulation, and
engineering interpretation of electric and magnetic field behavior in
practical systems.

## Overview

These projects were completed as part of upper-division coursework in
electromagnetic waves and fields.

Topics include electrostatics, electromagnetic propulsion, transmission
line modeling, and plane-wave reflection.

Each report integrates physical intuition with mathematical analysis and
computational modeling.

## Areas of Study

### Electrostatics and High-Voltage Systems
- Charge distribution on conductors
- Electric field breakdown
- Energy storage mechanisms
- Safety and environmental effects

### Electromagnetic Forces and Energy Conversion
- Lorentz force propulsion
- High-current magnetic fields
- Capacitor bank discharge modeling
- Mechanical and thermal limitations
- System safety considerations

### Wave Propagation and Transmission Lines
- Telegrapher’s equations
- Finite-Difference Time-Domain (FDTD) modeling
- Impedance matching and reflections
- Lossy and dispersive media
- Time-domain and frequency-domain behavior

### Plane-Wave Reflection and Refraction
- Fresnel reflection coefficients
- Brewster angle
- Critical angle and total internal reflection
- Dielectric interface analysis
- Polarization effects

## Featured Projects

### Electromagnetic Railgun Design and Analysis

This project analyzed a capacitor-driven railgun system using Lorentz
force principles and high-current circuit modeling.

Key elements included:
- Multi-kiloamp current estimation
- Magnetic field and force calculation
- Energy and power analysis
- Mechanical and thermal constraints
- Safety and feasibility evaluation

This project demonstrates large-scale EM energy conversion analysis and
system-level thinking.

---

### FDTD Transmission Line and Fresnel Modeling

This project implemented Finite-Difference Time-Domain simulations of
transmission lines and analyzed plane-wave reflection at dielectric
interfaces using MATLAB.

Key elements included:
- Time-domain wave propagation modeling
- Reflection and absorption analysis
- Loss and leakage modeling
- Brewster and critical angle extraction
- Numerical and analytical comparison

This project highlights strong computational electromagnetics skills.
