# High-Speed Digital and Signal Integrity

Graduate coursework in high-speed digital design, signal integrity, and
power integrity at CU Boulder, combining simulation in industry-standard
tools with measurement on real hardware.

## [Measurement Labs (ECEN 5534)](measurement-labs/)
**Scope, TDR, and VNA measurements correlated against simulation**

Time-domain and VNA measurements of coax and PCB interconnects, with
every result compared against a model and reported with its uncertainty.
Covers source impedance extraction, reflections and ringing, time of
flight and Dk extraction, virtual TDR from VNA S-parameters,
microstrip Z0 measurement compared against calculators and 2D field
solvers, and parasitic modeling of resistive terminations.

## [High-Speed Digital Design Labs (ECEN 5224)](labs/)
**ADS, HyperLynx, and SerDes Designer simulation**

Transmission lines, terminations, and TDR in ADS; differential pair eye
diagrams, skew, and termination in HyperLynx; and serial link channel
loss, via stubs, and transmit FFE in SerDes Designer, including a clean
15-inch channel reaching about 16.7 Gb/s with FFE versus 7.7 Gb/s with a
stub.
