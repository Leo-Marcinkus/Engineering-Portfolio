# Electromagnetics, Waves, and Fields

Applied electromagnetic modeling and simulation projects from
upper-division coursework, combining analytical field theory with
numerical simulation in MATLAB.

## [Electromagnetics Projects](projects/)
**Field Modeling, FDTD Simulation, Energy Conversion**

- **FDTD Transmission Line and Fresnel Modeling** - Finite-difference
  time-domain simulation of transmission lines, plus plane-wave reflection
  at dielectric interfaces with Brewster and critical angle extraction
- **Electromagnetic Railgun Design and Analysis** - Capacitor-driven
  railgun modeled with Lorentz force, multi-kiloamp current, and energy,
  thermal, and safety constraints
- **Van de Graaff Generator** - Electrostatic modeling, charge
  distribution, and field breakdown estimation
