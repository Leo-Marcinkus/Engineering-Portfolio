# Semiconductor Devices

Device characterization labs and research reports from ECEN 4002
(Advanced Electronic and Semiconductor Device Laboratory, Fall 2025),
covering diodes, optoelectronic devices, BJTs, MOSFETs, and advanced
transistor and laser architectures.

## [Gate-All-Around Nanosheet FET Scaling Analysis](research-reports/final-gaa-nanosheet/)
**Final Research Report**

Analysis of gate-all-around nanosheet transistors as the path for CMOS
scaling beyond FinFET: electrostatic control, drive current scaling with
stack count, and comparison against TreeFET and CombFET architectures.

📂 Project Folder
`semiconductor-devices/research-reports/final-gaa-nanosheet/`

## [Vertical-Cavity Surface-Emitting Lasers](research-reports/midterm-vcsel/)
**Midterm Research Report**

VCSEL structure and performance for high-speed optical links: DBR
reflectivity, cavity length tuning, and their effect on loss, beam purity,
and modulation bandwidth, with simulation results.

📂 Project Folder
`semiconductor-devices/research-reports/midterm-vcsel/`

## [Device Characterization Labs](labs/)
**SMU Measurement and Parameter Extraction**

Seven lab reports measuring real devices with a source-measure unit:
PN junction ideality factor and series resistance, LED and photodiode
behavior, solar cell fill factor and efficiency, BJT operating regions and
current gain, MOSFET threshold voltage and transconductance extraction,
and device-to-device variation statistics.
