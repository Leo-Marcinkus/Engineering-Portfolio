# Semiconductor Device Laboratory Reports

This directory contains experimental laboratory reports focused on the
electrical characterization, modeling, and analysis of fundamental
semiconductor devices.

These experiments emphasize quantitative measurement techniques,
parameter extraction, and correlation of experimental results with
device physics theory.

## Overview

The laboratory work in this section complements the research-based
midterm and final reports by providing hands-on experience with
real semiconductor devices and industry-standard test equipment.

Topics span PN junctions, optoelectronic devices, bipolar transistors,
and field-effect transistors.

All reports document experimental setup, data collection, analysis,
and engineering interpretation.

## Areas of Study

### PN Junction Devices
- Small-signal diode I–V characterization
- Temperature-dependent behavior
- Ideality factor and saturation current extraction
- Series resistance effects

### Optoelectronic Devices
- LED electrical and optical behavior
- Photodiode and solar cell characterization
- Maximum power point tracking
- Fill factor and efficiency analysis

### Bipolar Junction Transistors
- Operating region identification
- Current gain (β) measurement
- Common-emitter characterization
- Switching and amplification behavior

### Field-Effect Transistors
- MOSFET operating regimes
- Transfer and output characteristics
- Threshold voltage extraction
- Transconductance analysis
- Square-law model validation

### Measurement and Modeling
- SMU-based I–V sweeps
- Curve fitting and regression analysis
- MATLAB and Python data processing
- Instrumentation error evaluation

## Featured Experiments

### Photovoltaic and Photodiode Characterization (Lab 3)

This experiment investigated silicon photodiodes and solar cells using
a precision source-measure unit (SMU). Measurements included dark and
illuminated I–V curves, short-circuit current, open-circuit voltage,
fill factor, and power conversion efficiency.

Key techniques demonstrated:
- Automated SMU data acquisition
- Maximum power point extraction
- Non-ideal diode fitting
- Optical-to-electrical conversion analysis

This lab provides strong evidence of experimental rigor and
device-level modeling skill. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

### MOSFET Transfer Characteristics and Modeling (Lab 6)

This experiment focused on measuring MOSFET operating regimes and
extracting device parameters through transfer and output
characteristics.

Measured data was analyzed using square-law theory to estimate
threshold voltage and transconductance.

Key techniques demonstrated:
- Multi-bias I–V sweeping
- Transfer curve fitting
- Threshold voltage extraction
- Saturation and linear region analysis
- Model-to-data correlation

This lab highlights strong understanding of MOSFET physics and
measurement methodology. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}
