# Midterm Report - Vertical-Cavity Surface-Emitting Lasers (VCSELs)

**Course:** Advanced Electronic and Semiconductor Device Laboratory (ECEN 4002)  
**Date:** Fall 2025  

## Overview

This report presents a technical analysis of Vertical-Cavity Surface-Emitting
Lasers (VCSELs), focusing on their physical structure, operating principles,
and performance optimization for high-speed optical communication systems.

The paper reviews cavity design, distributed Bragg reflectors (DBRs), and
quantum well operation, and evaluates how structural parameters influence
loss, efficiency, and modulation bandwidth.

The full report is available in `LMarcinkusMidtermReport-4002.pdf`

## Technical Objectives

- Review VCSEL device architecture and fabrication structure
- Analyze DBR reflectivity and cavity length effects
- Examine recombination and stimulated emission mechanisms
- Evaluate bandwidth and efficiency tradeoffs
- Investigate methods for reducing optical and electrical losses
- Connect device physics to system-level performance

## Methodology

The report synthesizes published research literature and simulation results
to evaluate how structural parameters influence VCSEL performance.

Methods include:
- Literature-based comparative analysis
- Review of experimental performance data
- Simulation-based optimization studies
- Analytical evaluation of optical behavior

## Key Findings

- Optimized DBR reflectivity reduces cavity losses
- Cavity length tuning improves beam purity
- Structural optimization enables >50 Gb/s modulation
- Thermal management is critical for high-speed operation
- VCSELs enable scalable wafer-level photonic integration

## Results and Impact

The analysis demonstrates that careful optimization of DBR structures and
cavity geometry can significantly improve optical efficiency and bandwidth
while maintaining low threshold current.

These findings support the use of VCSELs in:
- Data center interconnects
- High-speed fiber links
- LiDAR systems
- Integrated photonic platforms

## Lessons Learned

- Device geometry strongly influences optical performance
- Tradeoffs exist between bandwidth, loss, and manufacturability
- Simulation is essential for photonic device optimization
- Thermal effects limit achievable modulation speed
- VCSELs provide superior scalability compared to edge-emitting lasers
