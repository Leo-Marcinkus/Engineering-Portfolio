# PCB Laboratory Experiments and Measurement Studies

This directory contains supporting laboratory reports focused on printed
circuit board (PCB) measurement, signal integrity, power distribution,
and mixed-signal validation techniques.

These experiments provided hands-on experience with industry-standard
instrumentation and established best practices for hardware testing,
data analysis, and design verification.

## Overview

The laboratory work in this section complements the full board-design
projects by emphasizing experimental methodology, controlled testing,
and quantitative performance evaluation.

Topics span power integrity, layout practices, trace characterization,
and measurement techniques.

## Areas of Study

### Power Integrity and Decoupling
- Effects of decoupling capacitor value and placement
- Power rail collapse and switching noise analysis
- Inrush current measurement and characterization
- Return path and grounding strategies

### Signal Integrity and Layout Practices
- Comparison of good and poor PCB layout techniques
- Rise/fall time degradation analysis
- Impact of return plane continuity
- Noise coupling mechanisms

### Trace and Interconnect Characterization
- Two-wire vs. four-wire resistance measurements
- Trace width and current-carrying capacity analysis
- Thermal and failure behavior under overload

### Measurement Techniques
- Single-ended vs. differential probing
- Instrumentation accuracy evaluation
- Noise injection and isolation studies
- ADC-based sensing validation

### Embedded Instrumentation
- Arduino-based data acquisition systems
- DAC/ADC interfacing
- Automated measurement logging
- Sensor integration and calibration

## Representative Experiments

Examples of completed studies include:

- Decoupling capacitor placement and MOSFET switching noise analysis
- PCB trace resistance and burnout testing
- Layout-dependent rise/fall time evaluation
- Power rail inrush current profiling
- Differential measurement validation
- Instrument droid breadboard prototyping

Each report documents experimental setup, measurement procedures,
results, and engineering analysis.

## Tools and Instrumentation

- High-bandwidth oscilloscopes
- Digital multimeters and current probes
- Function generators
- Programmable power supplies
- Arduino and ATmega-based platforms
- ADS1115 and auxiliary measurement hardware

## Documentation Standards

All laboratory reports follow formal technical documentation practices,
including:

- Clearly defined objectives
- Controlled experimental methodology
- Quantitative data presentation
- Comparative analysis
- Engineering conclusions

Supporting plots, scope captures, and calculations are included
in individual reports.
